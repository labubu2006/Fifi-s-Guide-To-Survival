Breakfast
Desi Omelette
A fluffy, spiced egg omelette packed with fresh onion, tomato, and green chili. Simple, quick, and packed with flavor.
Time: 10-15 Mins Serves: 1
Ingredients:
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/2 Onion
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Green Chili
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Red Chili Powder
  • Butter or Oil
Instructions:
Prep the Mixture:
  • Crack eggs and whisk. Finely chop onion, tomato, and green chili. Add spices.
  • ⚠️ Haalka haath rakhna (Don't add too much spice!)
Cook the Omelette:
  • Heat butter/oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Pour in egg mixture.
  • When edges set, simply fold in half and serve!
Breakfast
Aloo Wala Paratha
A classic Punjabi stuffed flatbread. Crispy on the outside, soft and spicy potato filling on the inside.
Time: 20-30 Mins Serves: 1
Ingredients:
  • 2 Potatoes
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 Green Chili
  • Garlic (optional)
  • Salt, Pepper
  • Red Chili, Haldi, Cumin
  • Dough & Ghee/Oil
Instructions:
Prepare the Filling:
  • Boil potatoes until tender. Mash them well.
  • Mix in chopped onion, chili, garlic, and all spices.
Cook the Paratha:
  • Stuff the dough, fold into a half-moon shape, and seal.
  • Pan-fry with ghee/oil on both sides until golden brown.
Breakfast
Egg & Cheese Quesadilla
A crispy, golden tortilla stuffed with fluffy scrambled eggs and gooey melted cheese. A 10-minute breakfast winner!
Time: 10 Mins Serves: 1
Ingredients:
  • 1 Tortilla
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/4 cup Shredded Cheese
  • Chili flakes / Hot sauce
  • Butter or Oil
Instructions:
Assemble:
  • Scramble eggs in a pan.
  • Place tortilla in an oiled pan. Add cheese, eggs, and chili flakes to one half.
  • Fold over. Cook both sides until golden and cheese is melted.
Lunch
Mediterranean Chicken Gyro Bowl
Juicy seared chicken over fluffy rice, loaded with fresh cucumber, tomatoes, olives, and feta. A healthy bowl of flavor.
Time: 30 Mins (Prep 15 / Cook 15) Serves: 2
Ingredients:
  • 2 Chicken breasts (cubed)
  • Olive oil, Lemon juice
  • Garlic, Oregano
  • Cooked Rice
  • Cucumber, Tomatoes
  • Olives, Feta cheese
  • Tzatziki sauce
Instructions:
Cook the Chicken:
  • Marinate chicken with oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  • Sear in a hot skillet for 6-8 minutes until fully cooked.
Assemble the Bowl:
  • Divide rice into bowls. Top with chicken, veggies, and feta.
  • Finish with a generous dollop of tzatziki sauce.

Classic Red Sauce Pasta
A simple, rich tomato sauce made with San Marzano tomatoes, fresh garlic, and basil coating your favorite pasta.
Time: 35 Mins (Prep 10 / Cook 25) Serves: 2
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups Rigatoni
  • 2 tbsp EVOO
  • 4 Garlic cloves
  • 28oz Crushed Tomatoes
  • Red pepper flakes
  • Fresh Basil
  • Parmesan cheese
Instructions:
Prepare the Pasta:
  • Cook pasta al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water.
Make the Sauce:
  • Saute garlic and flakes in olive oil for 1 minute.
  • Add tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15-20 mins.
  • Stir in fresh basil. Fold pasta into the sauce.
Lunch
Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo
Indulgent, creamy fettuccine tossed in a rich butter and parmesan sauce. Mushrooms are optional, but highly recommended!
Time: 25 Mins (Prep 10 / Cook 15) Serves: 2
Ingredients:
  • Fettuccine pasta
  • 8oz Mushrooms (opt)
  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 1 cup Heavy Cream
  • 3 Garlic cloves
  • 1 cup Parmesan
  • Parsley
Instructions:
Prep the Mushrooms:
  • If using, sauté mushrooms in butter until browned; set aside.
Make the Sauce:
  • Melt butter with cream over medium-low heat. Add garlic for 2 mins.
  • Whisk in cheese until smooth. Season.
  • Fold cooked pasta and mushrooms into the sauce. Garnish with parsley.

Stovetop Stuffed Bell Peppers
Tender bell peppers stuffed with a savory ground chicken and rice mixture, topped with melty cheese. No oven needed!
Time: 50 Mins (Prep 20 / Cook 30) Serves: 4
Ingredients:
  • 4 Bell Peppers
  • 450g Ground Chicken
  • 1 Onion, Garlic
  • 200g Cooked Rice
  • Corn, Tomato Sauce
  • Taco Seasoning
  • Cheese, Broth
Instructions:
Prepare the Filling:
  • Saute onion/garlic. Cook ground chicken until browned.
  • Stir in rice, corn, sauce, and spices. Simmer 2 mins.
Steam the Peppers:
  • Stuff peppers. Place upright in a deep skillet. Add broth to the bottom.
  • Cover and steam on medium-low for 20-25 mins.
  • Top with cheese, cover for 2 mins to melt.

Skillet-Only Moussaka
A Greek-inspired casserole layered with eggplant, savory meat sauce, and a creamy ricotta topping. All made in one skillet!
Time: 50 Mins (Prep 20 / Cook 30) Serves: 4
Ingredients:
  • 1 Large Eggplant
  • 450g Ground Chicken
  • 1 Onion, Garlic
  • 1 can Diced Tomatoes
  • Cinnamon, Allspice
  • 250g Ricotta
  • 1 Egg, Parmesan
Instructions:
Cook the Base:
  • Pan-fry eggplant slices until soft; set aside.
  • In same pan, cook meat with onion/garlic. Add tomatoes and spices; simmer.
Assemble & Steam:
  • Layer eggplant over the meat sauce in the skillet.
  • Spread ricotta/egg/Parmesan mix on top. Cover and cook on low 12-15 mins.
